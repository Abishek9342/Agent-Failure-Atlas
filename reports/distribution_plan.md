# Distribution Plan — Agent Failure Atlas 2026

A legitimate, no-fake-engagement launch plan. Synthesizes both
independent market-research rounds (`research/final_kaggle_market_research.md`,
`research/final_kaggle_market_research_v2.md`, `research/launch_decision.md`)
— no new research was run to produce this document, per this task's
explicit "do not start another research project" instruction.

## Recommended launch window

**Day:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday
**Date:** the nearest qualifying day at least ~3-7 days out — **Tuesday,
September 1, 2026** (or Wed Sept 2 / Thu Sept 3 as equally-supported
alternates)
**Time:** approximately 6:00–6:30 PM IST
**Timezone:** IST — chosen for its overlap with US Eastern morning
(~8:30–9:00 AM ET) and Central European afternoon (~2:30–3:00 PM CET) at
that moment, rather than the originally-considered Friday 11:00 AM IST,
which is late-night in the US and pre-dawn in Europe.

This is our best evidence-based launch window, reasoned from timezone
arithmetic and general (non-Kaggle-specific) engagement research — **not**
a claim of a proven-optimal Kaggle publish time. No Kaggle-specific
publish-timing study was found to exist publicly in either research round.

## First 30 minutes

- Publish the dataset at the chosen slot.
- Immediately verify the live page renders correctly — title, description,
  schema/columns, thumbnail, tags, license.
- Publish the pre-drafted external post(s) (`reports/launch_copy.md`)
  referencing the live Kaggle link — early engagement velocity is the
  most evidence-supported discovery lever both research rounds found.
- Personally download the dataset and run through the quick-start code as
  a fresh visitor would, to confirm nothing breaks post-publication.

## First 2 hours

- Monitor for comments/questions; respond promptly and substantively.
- Share once, genuinely, in any Slack/Discord/community the creator is
  already a legitimate member of — no cross-posting into unrelated
  groups, no repeated posting.

## First 6 hours

- Check whether the external posts are driving referral traffic.
- Engage with any comments or forks that appear.
- Do not manufacture engagement in any form — no fake accounts, no vote
  requests, no engagement pods, no bots. This is a hard rule, not a
  suggestion.

## First 24 hours

- Check views/downloads/votes against the **realistic** target ranges
  below — not the original, evidence-unsupported 200/50/5-in-24h figures.
- Do not treat modest engagement as failure. Both research rounds found
  documented real cases of a first Kaggle dataset from a new creator
  going months with near-zero votes — this is the normal case, not a
  signal that something went wrong.

## Days 2–7

1. Publish a short Kaggle discussion post (draft ready in
   `reports/launch_copy.md`) — highest-leverage, lowest-cost action per
   both research rounds, and reinforces the transparency positioning.
2. Keep responding to comments/forks promptly.
3. If the launch posts got genuine traction, consider one additional
   longer-form external piece (e.g. a dev.to/Medium technical write-up) —
   don't force this without a real signal that people are engaging.
4. Do not publish a reactive "v1.1" just to appear active. Only update
   the dataset for a genuine data-quality issue, never as a routine
   engagement tactic.
5. Begin light scoping (not execution) of a possible next series entry —
   both research rounds independently identified "Agent Tool-Use Atlas"
   as the strongest candidate (an active research area with no confirmed
   Kaggle-native equivalent) — but only after this release has had a
   genuine week to establish track record. No new dataset work this week.

## Channels

| Channel | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kaggle discussion | Primary — "What Is the Hardest AI-Agent Failure to Recover From?" | Post within days 2-3, not day 1, to give the dataset page itself the first moment of attention |
| GitHub | Short repository announcement / mirror | Supports long-term credibility and citability; not a primary traffic driver |
| LinkedIn | Technical launch post | Real, evidence-supported external-promotion channel for a zero-history Kaggle creator |
| X | Concise technical launch post | Same rationale as LinkedIn |
| Reddit | r/MachineLearning or r/datasets, once, respecting each community's self-promotion rules | Check each subreddit's own rules before posting; do not post to multiple unrelated subreddits |
| Personal network | Direct, honest shares to people who would genuinely find this useful | Legitimate and low-cost |

## Explicitly excluded — do not do any of the following

- Fake votes, fake downloads, or any form of engagement manipulation
- Bots or automated engagement of any kind
- Spam — posting to unrelated communities, repeated posting to the same
  community, or ignoring a community's stated self-promotion rules
- Misleading claims about the dataset's origin, scale, or novelty
  (including any "first ever," "largest," or "state of the art" framing
  that hasn't been independently verified — none currently appears
  anywhere in the published materials, and none should be added)

## Realistic targets (replace the original 200/50/5-in-24h framing)

| Metric | Conservative (organic only) | Realistic (organic + honest promotion) | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Views (week 1) | 20–60 | 100–300 | 300–600+ |
| Downloads (week 1) | 3–10 | 15–40 | 40–80 |
| Votes (week 1) | 0–2 | 2–6 | 6–12 |

Use the "Realistic" column as the actual internal success bar. The
original 200 views / 50 downloads in 24h and 5 votes in a week targets
are retained only as **stretch targets** (see
`research/launch_decision.md`) — no evidence found in either research
round supports them as a typical or even common outcome for a
zero-history creator publishing a niche technical dataset without a
pre-existing audience. Missing them is not, on its own, a failure signal.
